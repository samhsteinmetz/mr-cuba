# Photos for Mr. Cuba Website

Place your tour photos in this directory. The website will automatically use them in the gallery.

## Recommended Structure:
- Place all tour photos here
- Use descriptive filenames (e.g., `central-vietnam-mountain.jpg`, `motorbike-tour-sunset.jpg`)
- Recommended format: WebP or JPG
- Recommended size: 1920x1080 or larger for best quality

## Photo Categories:
- Hero/Introduction photos: Showcase photos of Mr. Cuba and his motorbike
- Gallery photos: Various tour experiences and locations
- Testimonial photos: Optional photos of happy customers
